# wuxia/ image sources

All images in this folder are crops of public-domain paintings from Wikimedia Commons,
re-toned to an ink-on-paper palette (grayscale levels mapped from #221d17 to #ebe1c8) and resized.
The paintings are centuries old; Wikimedia Commons marks the reproductions as public domain (PD-Art).
